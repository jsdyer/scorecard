How to read the scorecard

Each survey asks market participants for the policy interest rate they expect in specific months. The median is the middle response. The middle half spans the 25th to 75th percentiles. Each row has its own respondent count.

Error is the survey median minus the announced overnight target. A positive error means the forecast was higher. One basis point (bp) is 0.01 percentage point. A forecast qualifies only if the Bank had published it before the decision day. A survey with a future quarter as its horizon is outside the monthly comparison.

The last-public-forecast view counts each completed decision once. Hit rate counts exact matches at the Bank’s published precision. Mean absolute error averages the size of the misses and includes exact matches as zero.

Quarterly surveys can change before results become public. These figures describe survey respondents’ expectations, while the governing council makes the policy decision. Only forecast months with an unambiguous single decision can be scored.

Data behind the scores

Source: Bank of Canada. CoolCode matches Market Participants Survey question 2.1 to the Bank’s announced overnight target. Coverage starts with June 2025 decisions. A weekly refresh preserves older decisions as they leave the Bank’s recent table.
